## 2. STRICT VISUAL INTEGRITY GUIDELINES

1. **No Stock Image Substitution:**
   - Under no circumstances should generic overseas buses or foreign transit models be uploaded and represented as Metro Probati buses.
2. **True-to-Life Color Grading:**
   - Photography must preserve the authentic signature red and white livery of the Ashok Leyland fleet.
3. **Optimized Delivery:**
   - All photographic assets are served with appropriate responsive sizes, preloads on above-the-fold hero images, and lazy loading on downstream sections.

## 3. RECOMMENDED FUTURE PHOTOGRAPHIC SHOOTS

For subsequent iterations, the following authentic assets should be photographed on-site in Chattogram:

1. **Interior Passenger Cabin:**
   - 2x2 pushback velvet seat layout during daytime with natural lighting.
   - Front-row designated women and elderly passenger seats.
   - Regulated central standing aisle showing grab handles and headroom.
2. **Physical Ticket Counter Storefronts:**
   - Kaptai Rastar Matha Master Counter exterior sign.
   - Bahaddarhat under-flyover counter with representative in uniform.
   - Katgor Terminal counter adjacent to Jalal Market / Diagnostic Centre.
3. **Crew in Action:**
   - Certified bus supervisor using on-board mobile validator.
   - Driver in uniform operating the steering console with safety clearance.
