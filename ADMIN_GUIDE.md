# Metro Probati — Super Admin & Operations Guide

## 1. Accessing the Admin Console
- **URL**: `/admin`
- **Default Super Admin**: মো. তারেক মাহমুদ (`admin@metroprobati.com`)
- **Quick Access**: Click the role switcher in the header or visit `/login` and select "সুপার অ্যাডমিন".

## 2. Tabs & Capabilities

### Tab 1: বহর ও বাস পরিচালনা (Fleet Management)
- View all active fleet buses (e.g. `চট্ট মেট্রো ব ১১-০১৮৩`, `চট্ট মেট্রো ব ১১-০২৪৫`).
- Change bus duty status between `on_duty`, `idle`, `maintenance`, and `offline`.
- Monitor driver assignment, supervisor contact numbers, and battery status.

### Tab 2: কাউন্টার ও স্টপ (Counters & Stops)
- View and manage all 11 corridor stops from Kaptai Rastar Matha to Patenga Sea Beach.
- Inspect booth operational hours, verified contact numbers, and coordinates.

### Tab 3: ভাড়া নীতি কনফিগ (Fare Policy Matrix)
- Configure base minimum fare (`৳10`) and maximum corridor cap (`৳45`).
- Ensure all published tariffs comply with BRTA guidelines and owner association resolutions.

### Tab 4: টিকিট ও অডিট লগ (Bookings & Audit)
- Search bookings by unique reference code (`MP-YYYYMMDD-XXXX`).
- Verify payment states (`paid`, `sandbox_paid`, `unpaid`, `refunded`).
- Inspect passenger phone numbers and assigned seat numbers.

### Tab 5: রিপোর্ট ও হেল্পলাইন (Incidents & Helplines)
- Update primary hotline (`01798542469`) and advice line (`01918695599`).
- Review incident logs dispatched by passengers or supervisors regarding delays, overcrowding, or lost property.
